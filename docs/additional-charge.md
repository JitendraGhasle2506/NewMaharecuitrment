# Additional charge register

HR can open `/hr/additional-charge` directly or choose **Additional charge** on `/hr/reportingManager`.

Choose an active employee or active user without an employee record, select the additional role from the searchable list loaded from the roles table, optionally choose an active cell, and set the start date. **Cell is optional: blank means All cells**, covering the selected person's team across cells, including direct reports without a cell assignment. Department choices are not offered or accepted for new assignments. Historical department charges remain readable. The selected role is validated when saving. The end date, order reference, and responsibilities are optional. An ongoing assignment has no end date. The end date is inclusive.

Optionally select **Person being replaced**. The preview lists current direct reports within the chosen scope (one cell or All cells), excluding the charge holder. Saving captures that team as a separate membership snapshot. Explicit PRIMARY reporting takes precedence; cell Level 1 defaults apply only to employees without a PRIMARY override. The inherited direct reports retain their existing descendants, so their team branches follow them. Without a person being replaced, the charge records additional duties without transferring a team or approval authority.

During the inclusive effective dates, PRIMARY hierarchy and reporting-chain views show the captured reports under the charge holder. A report moved to another cell is no longer covered by a cell-specific charge; All cells charges continue across cell moves. Inactive/relieved charge holders are excluded from hierarchy views. Once the charge expires or is revoked, the overlay no longer applies and the original mappings apply again. No scheduler or destructive rewrite is needed. The snapshot does not automatically capture employees assigned to the original authority later; revoke and recreate the charge to refresh coverage.

Active charge holders have an **Additional charge** badge in the hierarchy. Open it to see additional roles, the person being covered, and the scope. The primary designation is preserved. Reassigned direct reports show **Additional-charge reporting**. Holders also appear in the HR hierarchy selector even when a charge has no replacement team. Scheduled/expired/revoked assignments remain available in the charge register, without an active hierarchy badge.

On `/employee/dashboard`, **Your additional charges** lists the signed-in employee's currently active roles, covered person, scope, dates, and responsibilities. The dashboard also embeds **My team hierarchy** below the profile, using the shared self-scoped chart with search, branch expansion, zoom, and additional-charge markers. The separate team page and leave/tour approval links remain available. Duties without a replacement explicitly state that no team or approval authority is transferred. The charge notice disappears when no charge is active; it reads fresh assignments at each dashboard visit. In **My reporting chain** on the separate team page, subordinates see an **Additional charge** marker on acting managers and **Reports under additional charge** on transferred reporting links. No email notification is sent.

The charge holder cannot replace themselves. All cells coverage conflicts with any cell-specific coverage for the same incumbent over overlapping dates; separate cell-specific scopes can coexist. Holder/incumbent identities are locked in a consistent order to serialize overlap checks. A holder needs an active employee record with an active user account to inherit a nonempty team and its approvals. A user without an employee record can still receive a charge without a team. When a subordinate covers an ancestor, the effective incoming reporting edge is detached if necessary to prevent a circular hierarchy; stored primary mappings remain unchanged.

The register is paginated, with Scheduled, Active, Expired, and Revoked statuses. HR can revoke an active or scheduled charge; its record and actor remain in history. To correct an assignment, revoke it and enter the replacement. Duplicate positions for the same person and scope cannot overlap. Different positions or scopes can run concurrently.

Employee eligibility follows the hierarchy's active-account and relieving rules. A linked user is resolved to their employee identity. Stored roles, reporting mappings, and cell authorities remain unchanged. Reporting Manager continues to display the stored primary assignments.

## Leave and tour approvals

Replacement charges also cover leave/tour approvals for the captured team and its current descendants **within the chosen scope**: one selected cell, or All cells when blank. A moved team root is no longer covered by a cell-specific charge. New and already-pending requests retain their original approver IDs; pending queues and decision authorization resolve the effective approver dynamically. The holder can act only at the replaced person's stored approval stage. Other managers/HODs keep their own stages, and the regular manager-to-HOD workflow is preserved. The replaced person cannot process a covered stage while active coverage is in effect. Expiry or revocation returns pending authority to the original approver immediately on the next request; no permanent role grant or approver-ID rewrite occurs.

The holder cannot gain approval authority over their own applications. Legacy requests without staged approvers are covered only where the replaced person also has actual legacy reporting authority; hierarchy membership alone does not grant approval rights. Approval scope uses fresh bulk projections per operation, with legacy-source lookups cached within that operation.

The user who actually makes each manager/HOD decision is stored separately. Processed-history queries include these decision actors so completed decisions remain visible to the holder after the charge ends. Employees can open **Leave & Tour Approvals** from their dashboard, or use `/hod1/leaveApprovals`. Mobile leave approvals use the same service-level authorization.

Deployment: restart/redeploy the application to apply post-schema Flyway migrations **V151** through **V154**. V151 creates `employee_additional_charge`; V152 adds replacement identities and the `additional_charge_team` membership snapshot; V153 adds separate manager/HOD decision-actor columns and history indexes to leave/tour applications; V154 permits an empty cell scope. Existing charges are not assigned a replacement or team automatically. Charges with replacement teams participate in approval coverage while active.
