MahaIT Face Attendance Application

Maharashtra Information Technology Corporation Limited (MahaIT)

Effective Date: June 1, 2025 | Version 1.0.0

Welcome to the MahaIT Face Attendance Application ("Application"), developed and operated by Maharashtra Information Technology Corporation Limited ("MahaIT", "We", "Us", or "Our"), a Government of Maharashtra Undertaking. We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of the personal and biometric data of all employees, contractual staff, and authorized personnel using this Application.

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, process, store, and protect your information when you use our mobile application for attendance tracking, leave management, and organizational verification.

1. Information We Collect

To provide secure, AI-powered attendance tracking and office geofence validation, the Application collects the following categories of information:

Data Category Data Elements Collected Purpose & Utility
Biometric Data Facial landmarks, live blink detection status, and 512-dimensional vector embeddings generated via MobileFaceNet (ArcFace). Identity verification during Check-In and Check-Out to prevent proxy attendance or photo-spoofing.
Location Data Real-time GPS coordinates (Latitude & Longitude) captured strictly at the moment of punch submission. Validating that the employee is within an authorized office geofence boundary.
Profile Information Employee Name, Employee Code, Email Address, Mobile Number, Designation, Department, Reporting Manager details, and Profile Photo. User authentication, account management, attendance history mapping, and Leave Management.
Device & Session Data Device Biometric Token (Fingerprint/Face ID unlock token), JWT Access Tokens, Device Model, and Operating System version. Secure session management, token refresh rotation, and local biometric app unlock.

2. Device Permissions Required

The Application requires explicit access to specific hardware features on your mobile device:

  • Camera Access (NSCameraUsageDescription): Used exclusively to capture a selfie image during Check-In/Check-Out and profile photo enrollment. No continuous background video recording occurs.
  • Location Services (NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription): Accessed only when you trigger a Check-In or Check-Out request to confirm proximity to an assigned office geofence.
  • Device Biometrics (NSFaceIDUsageDescription / Fingerprint): Used optionally for local quick application unlock without re-entering your password.
Privacy Assurance: Raw facial video streams and continuous GPS location tracking are NEVER recorded in the background. Location is captured exclusively during the exact moment you perform a punch.

3. How We Process and Use Your Information

MahaIT processes collected data strictly for official, government-authorized human resource and workforce management functions:

  • Verifying identity during Check-In and Check-Out via on-device AI face recognition.
  • Ensuring anti-spoofing via real-time passive blink detection.
  • Verifying that punches are recorded within authorized office boundaries.
  • Processing leave requests, comp-off validations, and monthly regularization reports.
  • Generating digitally signed PDF regularization summaries for reporting authorities.

4. Data Storage and Security Measures

MahaIT employs robust, enterprise-grade security standards to safeguard your data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communication between the Application and the backend server (https://mahahrseva.mahait.org) is encrypted using Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) / Transport Layer Security (TLS) HTTPS protocols.
  • On-Device Vector Computation: Face detection and 512-dimensional vector embedding generation occur locally on your mobile device via TensorFlow Lite, ensuring unverified images are not transmitted across the network.
  • Encrypted Token Storage: Session tokens and credentials are saved in protected, encrypted device storage.

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

MahaIT does NOT sell, trade, rent, or commercialize your personal or biometric data to any third parties. Your data is accessible solely to:

  • Authorized HR personnel and Reporting Managers within your respective Government Department or Organization.
  • MahaIT Technical Infrastructure Administrators for maintaining system integrity and service delivery.
  • Law enforcement or regulatory authorities only when mandated by law or court order under Indian Information Technology Acts.

6. Data Retention Policy

Personal and attendance data are retained in official government servers for as long as the employee remains active with the deployed department or as required under Government of Maharashtra record retention rules. Upon employee resignation or termination, mobile session tokens and device biometrics are revoked.

7. Rights of Employees

Employees have the right to:

  • View their complete attendance history, working hours, and leave application records in the Application.
  • Update their profile photo and re-enroll facial data with manager authorization.
  • Enable or disable optional device fingerprint/Face ID sign-in at any time via Profile Settings.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

MahaIT reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy as needed to reflect technical enhancements, security updates, or policy changes. Any revisions will be communicated through the Application or posted on official MahaIT portals.
