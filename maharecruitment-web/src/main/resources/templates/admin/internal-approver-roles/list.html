How this rule works

  • The approver must also be above the employee in the reporting hierarchy. A role alone does not give access to anyone's requests.
  • Managers without a selected role still see their team's requests, marked View only.
  • MAHAIT and other recruitment types keep their own approval route and are not affected.
  • A change applies to the next approve or reject action, including requests that are already pending. Users do not need to sign in again.
